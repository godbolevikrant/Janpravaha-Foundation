WHAT WE DO

Working across needs, building pathways.

From education and healthcare to environment and rural development, our focus is on inclusion and opportunity.

OUR FOCUS AREAS

Practical action for lasting change.

We aim to facilitate access, inclusion and opportunity through initiatives shaped by the needs of communities.

Baseline Survey & Rural Development

In collaboration with Vanrai Organisation, we conducted a baseline survey in Sheri Budruk to assess socio-economic and environmental conditions. Our analysis helps guide community-specific interventions focused on water conservation, improved farming practices, sustainable livelihoods and long-term rural development.

School Development – Parbhani

Supported by Maharashtra Natural Gas Limited (MNGL), Janpravaha Social Foundation improved infrastructure at Sant Gadgebaba Ashram Shala and Late Kamalabai Vadkute Secondary School, benefiting over 450 students. The initiative provided classroom benches, dormitory bunk beds and 50 computers, improving student comfort, digital literacy and learning opportunities.

Youth Skilling – Saksham Bharat

In partnership with BharatCares, Janpravaha Social Foundation implemented the Saksham Bharat project to prepare youth from economically weaker sections for careers in Banking, Financial Services and Insurance (BFSI). The Foundation mobilized, counseled and enrolled 60 youth in Pune, supporting student engagement, employment readiness and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Mission Karmayogi

As part of this Central Government pilot project, Janpravaha Social Foundation conducted a large-scale training needs assessment of over 2,500 government officials, including Talathis and Gramsevaks, across six talukas in Solapur district and four talukas in Dharashiv district. The survey aimed to identify skill gaps and support capacity building and professional development among government personnel.

Learn About This Project

School Development – Nanded

Supported by Maharashtra Natural Gas Limited (MNGL), Janpravaha Social Foundation is enhancing infrastructure across five schools and one junior college in Nanded, Maharashtra. Benefiting over 2,300 students, the initiative focuses on providing two-seater classroom benches and basic computer facilities to improve student comfort, concentration and digital literacy in rural and semi-urban communities.

Learn About This Project

Swachh Bharat Mission 2.0 (Urban)

In collaboration with Kushagra Innovation Foundation, Janpravaha Social Foundation coordinates awareness and waste management initiatives across 25 Urban Local Bodies and 1,146 schools in six districts of Maharashtra. Through trained facilitators, interactive sessions and practical demonstrations, the project promotes waste segregation and the 5R principles: Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Repurpose and Recycle. School waste segregation corners, regular monitoring and monthly reporting support sustained implementation and environmental awareness.

Learn About This Project

Mind Matters Initiative

Janpravaha Social Foundation conducted a three-day mental health awareness initiative in Khadakwadi, Pune, engaging approximately 200–250 girls. The sessions focused on youth mental well-being, emotional resilience, coping skills and reducing the stigma surrounding mental health. Through awareness activities and supportive discussions, the initiative encouraged young people to express their concerns and prioritize their emotional health.
