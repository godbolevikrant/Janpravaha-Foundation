WHO WE ARE

A people’s movement for positive change.

Janpravaha Social Foundation strives to understand the needs of different sections of society and help connect underserved communities with the opportunities, resources and support they need.

From education and healthcare to livelihood skills, rural development and rehabilitation, we believe lasting progress begins when people are heard, included and empowered to shape their own future.
