OUR PROGRAMS

Responding to real community needs.

Our work evolves in response to local priorities and partnerships. The focus areas below represent the foundation’s broad areas of interest.

Education & Learning

Encouraging access to learning, educational support and opportunities for children, youth and communities to build knowledge and confidence.

Healthcare Management

Promoting health awareness, connecting people with relevant resources and supporting initiatives that strengthen community wellbeing.

Skill Development & Enhancement

Supporting practical skill-building, capacity development and pathways toward greater livelihood readiness and independence.

Environmental Sustainability

Encouraging environmental awareness, responsible practices and community participation in protecting shared natural resources.

Rural Development & Infrastructure

Working to identify development gaps and support infrastructure, facilities and initiatives that improve quality of life in rural areas.

Rehabilitation & Social Inclusion

Facilitating support and renewed opportunities for individuals and communities facing barriers to participation and wellbeing.
